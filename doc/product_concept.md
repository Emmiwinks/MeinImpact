# Product Concept

MeinImpact is a weekly civic action assistant. It activates politically
interested but passive people by recommending one concrete action that can be
completed in roughly three minutes.

The original working title was Papilio. The product name is now MeinImpact.

## Problem

Many people are dissatisfied with politics but do not move from frustration to
action. Common patterns are signing petitions without learning what happened,
sharing social posts without follow-up, or assuming that meaningful political
participation requires too much time and has too little impact.

This leaves the quiet majority quiet while highly active political extremes gain
visibility and shape the discourse.

## Idea

MeinImpact is not a news app, a social network, or an opinion forum.

It recommends one action per week based on the user's topics, values, and the
current civic context. Each action includes a clear explanation of why it is
relevant now, why it matches the user, and what outcome can realistically be
tracked.

## Core Flow

1. The user creates a profile by selecting topics and answering value questions.
2. The app recommends a weekly action with transparent reasons.
3. The user completes the action with AI-assisted but editable text.
4. The app tracks the outcome and notifies the user when something relevant
   happens.

## Initial Action Types

- Letter to a member of parliament.
- Public question via Abgeordnetenwatch.
- Petition signature for official or trusted platforms.
- EU consultation comment.
- Letter to a local newspaper.
- Building plan public comment.
- State-level ballot initiative signature.
- Consultation response to a draft law.

## What MeinImpact Is Not

- It is not a campaign platform with its own political agenda.
- It is not a tool for mass amplification.
- It does not host its own petitions.
- It does not rank political positions as right or wrong.
- It does not hide why a recommendation was made.

## Neutrality Requirements

- Use official or clearly documented civic sources where possible.
- Keep scoring and prioritization documented and auditable.
- Show recommendation reasons to the user.
- Version changes to ranking and editorial rules.
- Filter discriminatory, anti-democratic, and unconstitutional content.

## Privacy Requirements

- Apply minimal viable data collection.
- Keep political profile data local whenever possible.
- Do not monetize or share political profile data.
- Treat action history, location, demographics, and generated drafts as
  sensitive data.
- Give users clear deletion and export options before production launch.
